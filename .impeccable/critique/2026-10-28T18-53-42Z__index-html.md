#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The interface is deeply grounded in the Paraguayan digital commerce landscape. The combination of local Guaraníes pricing (`Gs.`), SIPAP bank transfer / cash on delivery workflows, and direct WhatsApp checkout eliminates the friction of traditional card checkout systems ill-suited to Paraguay. The design adopts an Apple-grade dark minimalist canvas with hairline borders and crisp typography that feels authoritative and distinctly modern.

**Deterministic scan**: The automated `impeccable detect` scan reported **0 anti-patterns**. It flagged 23 advisory notices regarding mobile micro-typography (`0.68rem`–`0.72rem`) and category accent colors outside the primary tokens, none of which represent functional regressions.

**Visual overlays**: Preflight script injection executed cleanly; no high-severity visual anti-patterns or blocking layout issues were detected.

#### Overall Impression
Punto Prisma presents an exceptionally well-executed, high-trust storefront and digital agency surface. Mobile bottom sheets, tactile micro-animations on interactive elements, and tabular price alignments elevate it above standard e-commerce templates. The primary areas for polish include desktop power-user keyboard shortcuts and offline resilience for WhatsApp links.

#### What's Working
1. **Paraguayan Commerce-First Checkout**: Direct encoding of cart items into an organized WhatsApp order with SIPAP and local shipping options provides unmatched local relevance.
2. **Apple-Grade Aesthetic & Tactile Micro-Interactions**: Monochromatic dark surface (`#0B0C0E`), crisp 1px borders, subtle button heartbeat pulses, and swipe-down bottom sheet dismiss on mobile feel fluid and native.
3. **Harmonious Mobile Ergonomics**: Product details modal, pricing banners, and AI bullet lists are cleanly proportioned to remain visible and accessible above sticky bottom CTAs.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] No Keyboard Shortcuts for Desktop Power Users**:
  - *Why it matters*: Desktop visitors cannot press `/` to instantly focus search or `C` to toggle the cart drawer, slowing navigation.
  - *Fix*: Bind `/` keydown to focus `#searchInput` and `c` / `Escape` for `#cartDrawer`.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable delight index.html`
- **[P2] WhatsApp Checkout Lacks Network / Offline Fallback**:
  - *Why it matters*: If a user has popup blockers or temporary network loss when tapping WhatsApp checkout, no error message or fallback contact number is shown.
  - *Fix*: Wrap checkout trigger in error handling with a copyable phone number toast fallback.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable harden index.html`
- **[P3] Secondary Micro-Typography Drift from Strict Design System Ramp**:
  - *Why it matters*: 23 advisory lint flags identify hardcoded font sizes in mobile media queries rather than unified tokens.
  - *Fix*: Consolidate micro-font tokens (`--text-micro: 0.70rem`, `--text-nano: 0.68rem`) into `:root` in `DESIGN.md`.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable polish index.html`
