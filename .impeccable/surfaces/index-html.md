# Surface Brief: Main Surface (index.html)

Scope: Full single-page application (SPA)
Mode: Persuade
Target: index.html

## Job and audience
Consumers and businesses in Paraguay seeking immediate digital subscriptions (streaming, gaming, software keys), general physical/e-commerce products, or hiring custom web engineering services with pricing in Guaraníes (Gs.) and instant WhatsApp checkout.

## Outcome and proof
Visitor explores the catalog effortlessly, uses reactive category filters, adds items to an Apple-style slide-over cart with live Gs. calculations or buys instantly, and triggers formatted WhatsApp orders; or requests a custom website quote directly.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A unified local commerce and agency ecosystem that refuses clunky e-commerce templates and disconnected agency sites, marrying instant retail utility with bespoke software engineering in an Apple-grade minimalist canvas.

OWN-WORLD: Monochromatic neutral canvas (#0B0C0E ground, #15171C elevated cards, #F4F5F7 text) with precise 1px micro-borders (#262930), soft ambient elevation, crisp optical typography, and emerald accent (#10B981) reserved strictly for live status and WhatsApp actions.

STORY: Visitors enter a high-trust digital storefront, immediately identify verified subscriptions and physical items in local currency (Gs.), review agency tiers, and complete any transaction or inquiry in one tap via WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating frosted glass navigation header with Punto Prisma brand emblem and live cart counter pill; full-width hero with an expansive headline, dynamic dual-action buttons, and a live ticker proving instant digital fulfillment in Paraguay.

FORM: Apple-inspired minimalist unified portal, seed key d886fb97.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
