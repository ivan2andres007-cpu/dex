# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Single-page application (SPA) in lightweight static HTML, Vanilla CSS, and modern JavaScript.

## Users

- Customers in Paraguay purchasing digital subscriptions (streaming, gaming, software), activation keys, and general e-commerce goods.
- Businesses, entrepreneurs, and individuals seeking professional custom web development services.

## Product Purpose

Punto Prisma is a minimalist, Apple-inspired single-page web platform (SPA) that unifies a digital subscription/activation key marketplace, a general e-commerce catalog, and a bespoke web development service portal. It provides local buyers with transparent pricing in Paraguayan Guaraníes (Gs.) and direct checkout/inquiry routing via WhatsApp.

## Positioning

A hybrid local commerce and agency portal specifically tailored for the Paraguayan market—combining everyday digital subscriptions and physical store goods with premium custom web development services, presented with Apple-level minimalist polish and immediate WhatsApp engagement.

## Operating Context

- Executed as a self-contained single-page application directly accessible across mobile and desktop browsers with zero build-pipeline dependencies.
- Commercial transactions, order confirmations, and bespoke development inquiries route directly to WhatsApp.
- Financial figures formatted natively in Paraguayan Guaraníes (`Gs.`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Single-page architecture**: Unified static SPA running on modern standards (HTML5, Vanilla CSS, vanilla ES6+ JS).
- **Dual catalog**: Digital subscriptions & activation keys alongside physical/general merchandise.
- **Agency section**: Commercial showcase for custom web development services with capability breakdown and direct project consultation.
- **WhatsApp integration**: One-tap direct contact buttons with pre-filled context for specific products or custom website inquiries.
- **Currency**: Paraguayan Guaraníes (`Gs.`).

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: Punto Prisma.
- **Aesthetic**: Minimalist, sleek Apple-inspired design language (refined typography, generous whitespace, subtle depth/blur, premium understated elegance).
- **Language & Voice**: Spanish-first, professional, trustworthy, and direct.

## Evidence on Hand

- Greenfield repository initialized; product catalog, service tiers, and pricing structures defined directly within client-side state.

## Product Principles

- **Apple-Grade Precision**: Uncluttered visual hierarchy, immaculate typography, smooth interactions, and thoughtful details.
- **Zero Friction Commerce**: Clear pricing in Guaraníes (Gs.) and direct WhatsApp checkout paths eliminate barriers for local buyers.
- **Dual-Surface Cohesion**: Seamless harmony between instant retail purchases and high-value custom development services.
